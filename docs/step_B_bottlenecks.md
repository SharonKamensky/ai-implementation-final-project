# Step B – Business Bottlenecks Analysis

## Bottleneck 1 – Fragmented Customer Service Process

### Description
Customer inquiries arrive through multiple communication channels, mainly Outlook and WhatsApp Business. Service representatives must manually search several systems (Shopify, Priority and Monday.com) to collect customer information before they can respond.

### Root Cause
The organization's core systems are not integrated, forcing employees to manually transfer information between systems and perform repetitive searches.

### Business Impact
- Average response time: 24 hours.
- Approximately 500 customer inquiries per month.
- Average handling time: 45 minutes per inquiry.
- Estimated labor cost: ₪26,250 per month.
- 10 of the 15 negative customer reviews mentioned slow response or missing refunds.

### Why AI?
This process requires understanding unstructured customer messages, classifying inquiry types, extracting relevant information and supporting response generation. Traditional automation alone cannot perform these tasks reliably.

## Bottleneck 2 – Logistics and Delivery Errors

### Description
Approximately 15% of monthly deliveries contain an incorrect address or missing item, resulting in costly return trips and customer dissatisfaction.

### Root Cause
Shipment preparation relies on manual data transfer and manual verification without automated validation between business systems.

### Business Impact
- Approximately 1,200 deliveries per month.
- About 180 faulty deliveries each month.
- Average return-trip cost: ₪280.
- Estimated monthly loss: ₪50,400.

### Why AI?
AI could assist in validating shipment information and identifying anomalies before dispatch. However, solving this problem also requires operational and warehouse process improvements beyond software automation.

**Evidence from the client file:**
- Galit (HR Manager)
- Nir (Business Analyst)

## Selected Bottleneck

The project will focus on **Fragmented Customer Service**.

### Justification

This bottleneck was selected because:

- It is fully supported by evidence in the client file.
- It is the best fit for an AI-based solution.
- It can be realistically demonstrated using Make, Mailhook and Google Sheets.
- The solution clearly demonstrates AI capabilities such as classification, information extraction and response drafting rather than simple automation.
- The expected business impact can be measured using clear KPIs such as response time, handling time and operational cost.
